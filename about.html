Artist statement

Between the visible
and the imagined.

My work moves between painting, digital image-making, and constructed visual worlds. I am interested in how color, texture, bodies, and artificial forms can shift perception — turning familiar matter into something unstable, strange, or quietly alive.

Each work is part of an ongoing process rather than a closed statement. I combine material experiments, layered images, and intuitive decisions to build spaces where the organic and the synthetic overlap, and where beauty can feel both tender and unsettling.
